## Attendance

Students must maintain a minimum attendance of 85% in every course to be eligible for end-semester examinations.
Students with attendance between 75% and 85% may apply for condonation with a medical certificate.
Condonation requests must be submitted to the department office within 7 days of returning to class.
Students below 75% attendance are not permitted to write the end-semester examination for that course.

## Examinations and grading

Continuous internal assessment carries 40% of the course grade and the end-semester examination carries 60%.
A student must score at least 40% overall to pass a course.
Revaluation requests can be submitted within 5 working days after results are published, with a fee of Rs 750 per course.

## Identity cards

Students must carry their ID card on campus at all times.
A lost ID card can be replaced at the Student Services desk for a fee of Rs 300; replacement takes 3 working days.

## Library

Undergraduate students can borrow up to 4 books for 14 days. Postgraduate students can borrow up to 6 books for 21 days.
The late return fine is Rs 5 per book per day.

## Grievances

Academic grievances should first be raised with the class mentor. Unresolved grievances go to the Head of Department,
and then to the Student Grievance Committee, which responds within 10 working days.
